ToolScout aims to publish clear, practical software guides that help readers make informed decisions.

## Research and accuracy

Guides should distinguish practical advice from provider claims. Features, plan limits and policies can change, so verify important details against current official documentation.

## Comparisons and recommendations

Recommendations should reflect stated use cases and trade-offs. A comparison should not imply one tool is best for everyone. Where claims cannot be verified, they should be qualified or omitted.

## Updates and corrections

Content should be reviewed when meaningful changes are identified. Readers can report corrections or broken links through the [ToolScout GitHub repository](https://github.com/kimara254/toolscout/issues).

## Affiliate relationships

Some links may earn ToolScout a commission if a reader makes a qualifying purchase. This should not be interpreted as a guarantee that a product is right for every reader. See the site's affiliate disclosure.
