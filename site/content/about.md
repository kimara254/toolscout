ToolScout is a practical guide to discovering software and online tools for productivity, research, creative work, and managing projects.

## Our purpose

Choosing a tool can take time. Features, pricing, integrations, and limitations differ, and promotional claims do not always tell the whole story. Our goal is to help readers ask better questions and make informed choices.

## How to use our guides

- **Start with your needs.** Identify the task you want to solve before choosing a product.
- **Compare the details.** Check the features, limits, integrations, and costs that matter to you.
- **Verify current information.** Visit the provider's official website for up-to-date pricing, terms, and availability.
- **Consider the trade-offs.** A tool that suits one person or business may not suit another.
