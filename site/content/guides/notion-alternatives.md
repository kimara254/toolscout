Notion combines documents, databases and project organisation. If that flexibility feels excessive, choose an alternative based on the work you actually need to do.

## Consider a focused task manager

A dedicated task manager may be easier to maintain when your main need is capturing actions, setting deadlines and reviewing a daily list. Compare Todoist and other task-focused options using your own recurring tasks.

## Consider a visual project board

A board-based tool such as Trello can make task status and handoffs easier to understand when the workflow moves through clear stages.

## Consider a local knowledge base

Obsidian may suit people who want Markdown files and links between personal notes, especially when control over local files matters.

## Compare before migrating

Test one real project. Review collaboration, mobile use, offline needs, export formats, access controls, current plan limits and total cost. Keep a backup of your existing information before making a large move.
