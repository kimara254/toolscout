A content calendar should make the next action and publication status clear. You do not need a complex system to start.

## Create workflow lists

Try Ideas, Ready to Plan, In Progress, Review, Scheduled and Published. Adjust them to match your actual process.

## Create one card per item

Include a working title, target channel, audience, owner, deadline, draft link and checklist. Keep the brief short enough that people will read it.

## Use a repeatable checklist

Research, draft, fact-check, edit, prepare visuals, approve and publish. Change this list to match your editorial process.

## Review weekly

Identify blocked work, overdue approvals and the next batch of ideas. Verify current plan limits and integration permissions before adopting paid features.
