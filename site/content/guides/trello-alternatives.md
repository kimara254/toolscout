Trello is built around visual boards and cards. An alternative may be useful if your work needs more structured documentation, detailed project coordination or a simpler personal task list.

## If you need a flexible workspace

Notion combines pages, databases and task organisation. It may suit teams that want project information and documentation together.

## If you coordinate complex projects

Asana may be worth evaluating when multiple people, ownership and project dependencies need a shared view.

## If you mainly manage personal actions

A focused task manager such as Todoist may be a better fit than a board designed for team workflows.

## How to decide

Recreate the same project in each candidate tool. Compare setup effort, status visibility, collaboration, export options, accessibility, current plan limits and total cost. Do not migrate everything until the trial works for your team.
