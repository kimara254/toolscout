A small team usually needs a few dependable capabilities rather than a separate application for every task.

## Cover four needs

1. **Communication:** a clear place for discussions and decisions.
2. **Work tracking:** shared tasks, owners and deadlines.
3. **Documentation:** searchable procedures and reference material.
4. **File storage:** organised files with sensible access permissions.

Some products cover more than one need. That can reduce cost and context switching, but a single platform is not automatically the best fit.

## Audit what you already use

Record each tool's purpose, owner, cost, essential integrations and duplicated information. Remove overlap only after checking integrations and export options.

## Set simple operating rules

Agree where tasks live, where decisions are recorded, how files are named and who manages access. Good conventions often matter more than extra features.
