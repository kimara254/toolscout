A workspace audit helps uncover clutter, duplication and information that is hard to retrieve.

## Review your tools

For each application, record its purpose, owner, cost, essential integrations and whether it stores sensitive information.

## Clean up files and notes

- Identify duplicate or outdated material.
- Choose consistent folder and naming conventions.
- Archive completed projects.
- Confirm important files have recoverable backups.
- Test search and retrieval with real examples.

## Reduce notification noise

Disable notifications that do not require timely action. Keep essential alerts for deadlines, approvals and operational issues.

## Document the workflow

Write down where new work enters the system, who owns it, how it gets reviewed and where completed work is stored. Recheck after a month and simplify rules that create more effort than they save.
