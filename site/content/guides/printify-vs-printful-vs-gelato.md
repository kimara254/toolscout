Choose a print-on-demand provider based on your products, customer locations and quality requirements—not just a headline price.

## Compare these factors

- Product range and print methods.
- Production location and delivery estimates for your market.
- Sample quality and consistency.
- Shipping costs and fulfilment arrangements.
- Store integrations and order management.
- Branding and packaging options.
- Refund, reprint and support policies.
- Current plan fees and transaction costs.

## Order samples and calculate total costs

When feasible, compare samples of the same or similar products. Use the same product, destination and order assumptions when comparing providers. Verify current prices and policies directly because they can change.
