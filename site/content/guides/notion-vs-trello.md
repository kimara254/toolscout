Notion and Trello can both help organise work, but they approach it differently. The better choice depends on whether you need a flexible workspace or a straightforward visual task board.

## Choose Notion when

- You want notes, documents, databases and projects in one workspace.
- Your process requires custom pages and interconnected information.
- You want to build a knowledge base alongside your tasks.
- You are comfortable spending time designing your workspace.

## Choose Trello when

- You prefer boards, lists and cards.
- Your team needs to see task status at a glance.
- You want a workflow that is easy to explain to new users.
- A clear visual process matters more than extensive customisation.

## Test the same project in both

Create the same tasks, deadlines, owners and reference notes in each product. See how much effort it takes to keep the workspace accurate after setup. Check current plan limits, collaboration features, integrations, privacy terms and export options before moving important work.

## Verdict

Consider Notion for a flexible information hub and Trello for a visual workflow. Test both with a small real project before migrating your entire operation.
