## About this policy

This page describes privacy considerations when visiting ToolScout. The site may change over time, so this policy should be reviewed whenever new services, analytics, advertising, or interactive features are introduced.

## Information you provide

ToolScout does not currently provide a contact form or user account registration on this website. Do not send sensitive personal information through public channels associated with the site.

## Saved guides and browser storage

The site's saved-guide feature may use your browser's local storage to remember the guides you select. This information is stored by your browser on your device and may remain there between visits. It is not the same as an account-based synchronised list.

You can clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing it may remove saved guides.

## Hosting and technical information

ToolScout is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit a hosted website, the hosting provider may process technical information associated with requests, such as information needed to deliver and secure the site. Consult [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for information about GitHub's handling of personal data.

## External websites

ToolScout links to external websites, including software providers and, where identified, affiliate destinations. Those websites have their own privacy practices and terms. Review their policies before submitting personal information or making a purchase.

## Affiliate links

Some links may generate a commission for ToolScout when a qualifying action occurs. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/toolscout/disclosure/) for more information.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or practices change. The date shown at the top indicates the most recent stated update.

## Questions

A dedicated privacy contact address is not currently published on this site. This page does not constitute legal advice.
