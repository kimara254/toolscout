## What is Printify?

Printify connects online sellers with a network of print providers offering customizable products. Sellers can select products, upload designs, review available printing options, and connect their workflow to supported sales channels.

Printify is not the same as a marketplace that automatically supplies customers. You still need to decide what to sell, attract buyers, provide accurate product descriptions, and deliver good customer service.

## How does print-on-demand work?

A typical workflow looks like this:

1. **Choose a niche.** Consider a specific audience, interest, profession, or hobby rather than trying to sell everything to everyone.
2. **Research products.** Compare available product types, print areas, provider locations, production estimates, and shipping options.
3. **Create original designs.** Use artwork and wording you own or have permission to use. Avoid unauthorized logos, copyrighted characters, and protected brand names.
4. **Prepare product listings.** Use clear product photos or mockups, accurate sizing information, and honest descriptions.
5. **Connect a sales channel.** Check which integrations and selling channels are supported for your chosen setup.
6. **Review a sample.** Where practical, order a sample to evaluate print quality, materials, sizing, and packaging before promoting the product.
7. **Monitor orders and costs.** Track production costs, shipping, platform fees, refunds, advertising, and other expenses.

## What should you compare before choosing a provider?

Print providers can differ in product availability, print quality, production times, shipping costs, and delivery destinations. Compare the options available for your intended customers rather than assuming one provider is best for every product.

Check these details before publishing a listing:

- Product and printing costs.
- Shipping fees and estimated delivery windows.
- Available sizes, colours, and print methods.
- Return, replacement, and damaged-item policies.
- Customer support and order tracking.
- Integration requirements and any subscription charges.

These details can change, so verify current terms on the provider's website before making business decisions.

## How do you estimate your profit?

Use a simple calculation before setting your selling price:

**Estimated profit per order = selling price − product cost − shipping you pay − selling fees − other variable costs.**

For example, if you sell an item for $25 and your combined product, shipping, and selling costs are $17, the remaining $8 is a contribution toward other expenses and profit. This is an illustration, not a current Printify price quote.

Remember to account for refunds, discounts, taxes where applicable, and customer acquisition costs. Revenue alone does not tell you whether a product is profitable.
