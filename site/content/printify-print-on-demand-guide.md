## What is Printify?

[Printify](https://printify.com/) is a print-on-demand platform that connects sellers with print providers offering customisable products. Depending on the available catalogue and provider, products may include T-shirts, hoodies, mugs, accessories, and more.

You choose a product, prepare a design, create a listing, and connect an eligible sales channel. When an order comes in, the fulfilment process depends on your chosen provider and setup.

**One important distinction:** Printify does not automatically bring you customers. You are still responsible for your product ideas, marketing, accurate listings, and customer service.

## What should you compare?

Do not assume every print provider is equally suitable. Compare the actual product and delivery options available for your target market.

- **Product quality:** materials, print method, available colours, and sizing.
- **Fulfilment:** provider location, production estimates, and delivery destinations.
- **Shipping:** fees, estimated delivery windows, and tracking.
- **Policies:** damaged products, replacements, returns, and customer support.
- **Store setup:** integrations, subscription costs, and transaction or marketplace fees.
- **Product presentation:** mockup quality, listing requirements, and the ability to show the product accurately.

Verify current details directly with [Printify](https://printify.com/) and its [Help Center](https://help.printify.com/). Features, prices, and terms can change.

## Calculate your costs before setting a price

![A coffee mug as an example of a product that can inspire a customised merchandise idea](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1514228742587-6b1558fcca3d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=85)

### A simple profit check

**Estimated amount remaining per order = selling price − product cost − shipping you pay − selling fees − other variable costs.**

For example, if an item sells for **$25** and product, shipping, and selling costs total **$17**, the remaining **$8** contributes toward other expenses and profit. This is an illustration—not a current Printify quote or a guarantee of earnings.

Also consider advertising, discounts, refunds, taxes where applicable, currency conversion, and the time you spend managing the store. Your actual profit may be lower.

## Who might find Printify useful?

Printify may be worth investigating if you:

- Want to test custom merchandise without buying inventory in bulk.
- Have a clear niche or an audience you understand.
- Want to compare products and fulfilment providers.
- Are prepared to handle marketing, listings, and customer support.

It may be less suitable if you need guaranteed sales, complete control over manufacturing, or delivery times that your chosen provider cannot meet.
