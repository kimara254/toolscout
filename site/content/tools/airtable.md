Airtable combines a spreadsheet-like interface with structured records and multiple ways to view information. It can suit teams that need more structure than a conventional spreadsheet.

## Consider it if

- You manage structured records with related fields.
- Different views help different people use the same information.
- Your workflow benefits from forms, linked records or automation.

## Check before choosing

Review record and automation limits, permissions, export options, integrations, privacy terms and total cost. Test with representative data before moving a critical process.
