## What is Obsidian?

A note-taking application that helps you organize connected ideas and personal knowledge.

## Key features


- Create linked notes

- Build a personal knowledge base

- Organize information in a local vault


## Who is it for?

People who want to connect research, notes, and ideas.



## Where to learn more

Visit the official website to check the current features, plans, and terms before deciding whether the tool suits your needs.

## How Obsidian organises information

Obsidian is built around notes stored in a vault. Its internal links can connect related notes, making it useful for research, learning, writing, and personal knowledge management.

A simple system can begin with ordinary notes and folders. Links and tags can then connect ideas across those folders without requiring a complicated structure.

## What to evaluate

- **Local files:** Understand where your vault is stored and how you will back it up.
- **Device access:** Check how you will use the same notes across your computers or mobile devices.
- **Sync and publishing:** Verify whether any paid service or third-party setup is needed for your intended workflow.
- **Plugins:** Community plugins can add capabilities, but review permissions, maintenance, and trust before installing them.
- **Portability:** Test that you can open and back up your notes in the format you expect.

Consult the [official Obsidian website](https://obsidian.md/) for current product details, features, and pricing.

## A simple workflow for beginners

1. Create a vault for one subject or project.
2. Write short notes in your own words.
3. Link notes when they refer to related ideas.
4. Use a few meaningful folders or tags rather than designing a complex system immediately.
5. Back up the vault and test that you can restore it.

## Advantages and trade-offs

Linked notes can help you discover relationships between ideas, and locally stored files can give you direct control over your notes. On the other hand, synchronisation, collaboration, publishing, and advanced workflows may require additional setup. Your ideal arrangement depends on the devices and features you need.
