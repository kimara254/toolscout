Asana is a work-management platform for coordinating tasks and projects. It may be useful when teams need shared ownership, deadlines and a common view of project progress.

## Consider it if

- Work involves multiple people and dependencies.
- Owners and deadlines need to be visible.
- Teams need a shared project overview.

## Check before choosing

Review current plan limits, reporting and automation features, access controls, integrations, data export and total cost for your team. Trial one real project before migrating every workflow.
