Todoist focuses on capturing tasks, organising projects and keeping track of due dates. It may suit people who want a dedicated task manager rather than a full documentation platform.

## Consider it if

- You need a quick way to capture and review actions.
- Recurring tasks and deadlines are central to your workflow.
- You prefer a focused task list over a highly customised workspace.

## Check before choosing

Compare current plan limits, collaboration features, integrations, reminders, export options and privacy terms. Test the workflow with a real week of tasks.
