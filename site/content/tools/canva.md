Canva is a browser-based design platform for creating visual materials such as presentations, social graphics and marketing assets.

## Consider it if

- You need to produce common visual formats quickly.
- Templates and collaborative editing fit your workflow.
- You want a lower learning curve than a specialist design application.

## Check before choosing

Review current plan limits, licensing terms for assets, export formats, brand controls, collaboration features and privacy settings. Confirm that any design assets are licensed for your intended use.
